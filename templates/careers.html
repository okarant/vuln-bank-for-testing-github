Open Positions

Security Engineer

Engineering

We're looking for someone to own our security strategy. You'll work closely with engineering and product teams to ensure our platform meets the highest security standards.

Responsibilities:

  • Define and maintain security policies and best practices
  • Conduct code reviews with a focus on secure coding patterns
  • Lead incident response and post-mortem processes
  • Collaborate with engineering on threat modeling and architecture reviews

Requirements:

  • 3+ years of experience in application security or security engineering
  • Familiarity with OWASP guidelines and secure development lifecycle
  • Experience with Python, Flask, or similar web frameworks

Backend Developer

Engineering

Join our team of developers building the future of digital banking. You'll work on APIs, data pipelines, and core banking logic that powers the VulnBank platform.

Responsibilities:

  • Design and implement RESTful APIs and GraphQL endpoints
  • Build and maintain database schemas and query patterns
  • Write unit and integration tests for critical banking flows
  • Participate in code reviews and architecture discussions

Requirements:

  • Proficiency in Python (Flask preferred) and SQL
  • Experience with PostgreSQL and Docker
  • Understanding of RESTful API design and authentication patterns
  • Strong problem-solving and communication skills

DevOps Engineer

Infrastructure

Help us build and maintain reliable, scalable infrastructure for our banking platform. You'll own the CI/CD pipeline, container orchestration, and monitoring stack.

Responsibilities:

  • Manage Docker-based deployment pipelines
  • Set up and maintain monitoring, alerting, and logging infrastructure
  • Optimize application performance and resource utilization
  • Ensure high availability and disaster recovery readiness

Requirements:

  • Experience with Docker, Docker Compose, and CI/CD tools
  • Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
  • Strong scripting skills (Bash, Python)

Customer Support Specialist

Support

Be the first point of contact for our customers. This role requires patience, empathy, and a genuine desire to help users get the most out of VulnBank.

Responsibilities:

  • Respond to customer inquiries via email, chat, and phone
  • Troubleshoot account and transaction issues
  • Escalate technical problems to the engineering team
  • Contribute to our knowledge base and FAQ documentation

Requirements:

  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Experience in customer support, preferably in fintech or banking
  • Comfortable working with ticketing systems and CRM tools
